## Full description
Listen to 45,000+ live radio stations from every country on Earth — one beautiful app, zero sign-up.

KEY FEATURES
• 45,000+ verified live stations from radio-browser.info + radio.garden combined coverage
• Find your sound in seconds: search by name, country, language or genre
• Phone-app style navigation: Trending, Top Voted, Favourites, Recent
• Heart anything — favourites persist on your device, no account needed
• Save stations for offline listening (perfect for flights & fringes)
• Sleep timer so the music goes to bed when you do
• Data saver mode for metered connections — low-bitrate streams only
• Similar-station suggestions if a stream is dead on arrival
• Share any station with a direct link
• Works on every screen size, installs like a native app
• Dark & light themes

NO ACCOUNT. NO TRACKING. NO ADS.
Your favourites and history stay on your device only. Full privacy policy: radiobeast site /privacy.
